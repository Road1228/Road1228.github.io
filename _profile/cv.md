## Research Profile

Doctoral researcher at the University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), conducting research at the Institute of Plasma Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ASIPP). Research focuses on machine learning for EAST plasma equilibrium reconstruction, feedforward coil-current trajectory design, diagnostic-data reconstruction, and uncertainty-aware prediction. First-author research published in *AIP Advances* and presented at international conferences.

## Research Experience

### Fast Plasma Equilibrium Reconstruction on EAST

<p class="cv-meta"><span class="cv-tag">Published</span> <em>AIP Advances</em> (2023), first author</p>

- Developed a deep-learning approach mapping magnetic measurements to two-dimensional poloidal magnetic flux, supervised by offline EFIT reconstructions.
- Used automated hyperparameter optimization and evaluated predicted flux surfaces, the last closed flux surface, and normalized internal inductance against EFIT.

### Physics-Informed Imitation Learning for Feedforward Coil-Current Design

<p class="cv-meta"><span class="cv-tag">Manuscript submitted</span> <em>Communications Physics</em>, first author</p>

- Investigate physics-informed imitation learning and Seq2Seq Transformer methods for EAST feedforward coil-current trajectory design, focusing on temporal modeling and physical constraints.
- Invited presentation at AAPPS-DPP 2026 and oral presentation at an IAEA technical meeting.

### Diagnostic Reconstruction and Uncertainty-Aware Prediction

<p class="cv-meta"><span class="cv-tag">Collaborative research</span> <em>Nuclear Fusion</em> (2025)</p>

- Co-authored research on reconstructing missing electron temperature using time series extrinsic regression (*Nuclear Fusion*, 2025; third author).
- Co-authored research on Bayesian neural networks for energy confinement time prediction and uncertainty quantification (*Nuclear Fusion*, 2025; fifth author).
